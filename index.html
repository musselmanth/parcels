How to play

  • Split the whole board into parcels of 3 connected tiles: a line, a bend, or a little triangle.

Every parcel

  • Shape: all the same or all different.
  • Color: all the same or all different.
  • Count: 1 to 3, all the same or all different.
  • A parcel can follow the rules and still be wrong. Only one way of splitting the board works.

Score

  • Up to 1000 points: beat the board's par time, make fewer mistakes, and pop fewer parcels. Harder levels forgive more slips.

Controls

  • Swipe across 3 tiles (or tap them) to make a parcel.
  • Swipe across 2, or tap 2 tiles and then tap either one again, to pencil in a pair when you're not sure of the third. Tap an empty spot to clear a selection. Later, swipe from the pair onto a third tile, or tap the pair and then the third tile. Tap a pair twice to erase it.
  • Tap a parcel to pop it.
  • Press and hold a parcel to lock it when you're sure. Taps won't pop a locked parcel; press and hold it again to pop it.
