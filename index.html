How to play

Tap any example to try it yourself. Scroll down for the rules, the controls and scoring.

Split the whole board into parcels: groups of 3 touching tiles. Every tile ends up in exactly one parcel.

A parcel can be a line, a bend or a triangle.

The rule

Every tile has a shape, a color and a count (1, 2 or 3 symbols). In a parcel, each of the three has to be all the same or all different. If two tiles match and the third doesn't, it's not a parcel.

Only one answer

A parcel can follow the rule and still be wrong. Each board splits only one way, so if you get stuck, pop a parcel and look again.

Controls

Make a parcel Swipe across 3 tiles, or tap them one at a time. Tap an empty spot to clear a selection.
Pencil in a pair Not sure of the third tile? Swipe across 2, or tap 2 and then tap one again. Add the third later. Tap a pair twice to erase it.
Pop a parcel Tap a parcel to break it back into loose tiles.
Lock it in Press and hold a parcel when you're sure. Taps won't pop it; hold again to pop it.

Score

  • Up to 1000 points and 5 stars: beat the board's par time, make fewer mistakes, and pop fewer parcels. Harder levels forgive more slips.
  • Misses counts the parcels you tried that broke the rule.
  • Stuck? Solution shows the answer, but that board scores 0 and can't be replayed.
