How to play
  • Split the whole board into 12 parcels of 3 tiles. Tiles in a parcel must be connected: in a line, a bend, or a little triangle.
  • Shape: all the same or all different.
  • Color: all the same or all different.
  • Count: all the same or a run: 1·2·3 or 2·3·4. (1·2·4 doesn't count.)
  • Tap 3 tiles to make a parcel. Tap a parcel to break it up. A parcel can follow the rules and still be wrong: only one way of splitting the board works.
